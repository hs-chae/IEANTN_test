## Comparison policy

The intervening original-source envelopes are not thrown out: the comparison takes the pointwise maximum for lower bounds and intersection/minimum for upper bounds across the complete inherited and new valid branches on the exact domains. Proofs compare clipped spectral branches on whole domains without sampling and provide matched coefficient/onset comparisons, including reconstructed B0 alternatives using the improved method. A paired reconstruction is not an independent blind baseline. No claim is made about an optimum over arbitrary as-yet-undiscovered deductions of the original papers, or about the sharpness of spectral majorants for the arithmetic errors.
