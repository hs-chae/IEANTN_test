## Serious improvements attempted and limitations

* **Do not drop QRH.** Using a zero-free region of Vinogradov–Korobov type alone or comparing B1 just against \(0.2/L^3\) would hide most of the actual B0 strength. The paired density construction uses both finite RH and QRH at its exact \(7/8\) boundary. The ordinary direct input retains two powers of \(L\). Application of the Mellin logarithmic derivative instead gives the sharper \(27/32\) boundary; splitting at \(3/4\) yields a convergent weighted high-zero sum, removing the two log powers. A one-bin estimate at the cap without weighting by height substantially overestimates the useful middle range; this motivated the grid and Abel telescoping. Early trials at \(s=0.65\) with the rough exponential replacement for \((h+v)^q\) even have \(1-k-q/h<0\), and were discarded. The cubic plus concavity bound is valid and avoids that defect for the actual \(3/4\) split.
* **Ramanujan smooth main term.** Separate asymptotic upper/lower polynomials lose higher-order terms when the shifted count and square are subtracted. The squared-source ODE avoids the repeated subtraction loss; the exact order-27 comparison succeeded at 34 while trials at order 28 and larger with the same start failed an initial/comparison check. This is a start-dependent bound, not a limit on attainable orders. Unenclosed exploration suggested lower \(H_{10}\) cutoffs around 351–352 on B0; the certificates at those choices failed. The successful replacement is the monotone higher-order tail table, including \(359\) for that row. The exact smooth curve is retained in addition to all finite polynomials. The remaining transition error near the finite-RH floor comes from unknown high-zero contributions, not a main-term truncation shortage.
* **Finite sign boundary.** The published real left endpoint must not be read off the largest integer counterexample. The quotient sieve detects the precise non-integer crossing in that unit interval. The finite sign computation through \(1.15\cdot10^{16}\) remains inherited and was not independently re-sieved. First the elementary finite-RH theta transport did not cover the short join on its own; then the CH2 Cor.9.3 optimized-height psi bound, with prime powers subtracted for theta, repaired the join at \(e^{36.9}\). This also lowers early constant-margin onsets. These are B0 gains; applying the new tail to a genuinely finite obstruction would not help.
* **Mertens transport.** Simply applying absolute partial summation pays \(1+1/(1-b)\) to the leading term. Passing the absolutely convergent high-zero component through the weighted identities first removes this loss. In contrast, integrating the CH2 constant plateau to infinity in an unweighted \(dv\) tail is not valid; the switched tail or the legitimate \(dv/v^2\) route repairs this. The early inverse-log constants were first limited by a sieve up to \(10^8\) or \(4\cdot10^8\) joined directly to the finite-RH theta/psi bounds (roughly .15 or .08 respectively). At \(2\cdot10^9\) a direct join still paid about .048 in a coefficient. The anchored sign-sensitive bridge and CH2 at a much *lower* height \(10^7\) for the weighted sum, used also as a far endpoint, reduce the verified coefficient to .0223. The bridge floor near \(L=23.3\) is roughly .0221 even with the new B0 route; \(.02\) is not established on the corresponding whole rays. Extending certified weighted anchor data or improving the local \(\psi/\theta\) interval is a concrete next step. D02's extremely distant effect is not the principal limitation there.
* **Asymptotic bottleneck.** No result here excludes zeros on the respective cap lines. The constants depend on the weighted density majorant near the cap, and further improvements within this family could come from finer cut/parameter optimization or verified zero data/cancellation. A finite grid or a successful inequality at one \(x\) cannot establish optimality. For both applications the complete pointwise prescription takes the improved branch together with all preserved old bounds. Numerical comparisons are to strong explicit B0 reconstructions and matched original-source bounds; they are not a comparison to an unknowable optimum over every proof using the originals.
