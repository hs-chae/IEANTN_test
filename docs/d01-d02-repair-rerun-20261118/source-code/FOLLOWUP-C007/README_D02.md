# D02 submission guide

Start with D02_PROOF.md: §1 proves the D02 spectral edge and divergent basic/density estimates; §2 gives the Ramanujan profiles, §3 the Mertens products and prime sums, and §4 records limitations. In the new receipts, index 0 means B0, and index 1 means B0 plus the exact INPUT_CONTRACT.json assumption. The original-source-minus-D02 comparison is a within-trajectory reconstruction, not a blind baseline.

The inherited proof files ramanujan.md, mertens.md, foundation.md, and repair.md plus their original executable/evidence dependencies are retained under their prior names, so their links work. OWN_D01_CANDIDATE_LOCK.json is the old manifest; the new manifest is LOCK.json. Historical status and accounting files concern D01, not the new stage. OLD/withdrawn material in prior_* is not a premise; use OWN_D01_VALIDATED_FLOOR.json. The own research floor and its stated trust are unchanged. In particular, neither the false literal early real statement from the PT PNT paper nor the withdrawn single-log transcription of the finiteRH pi estimate is used.

New reproducibility: run /bin/bash --noprofile --norc ./d02_replay.sh in this directory. The checker also accepts individual subcommands (see d02_replay.sh). Proof-side formulas establish coverage and infinite tails independently of numerical search; the executable uses outward 52-digit Decimal bounds (300 at Taylor anchors). Decimal exp/log are correctly rounded, then each endpoint is widened by one representable step. Purely diagnostic point values in the receipts do not establish any continuous range. The old C++/Python certificates and published prime checks remain at their inherited trust; this is not full analytic Lean or source-first PV, nor an independent review of the new results.
