# Rejected attempts and limitations

- Do not substitute zero for the Gaussian epsilon or extrapolate an eventual positive count margin to its leading target. The fixed-width proof uses the affine roots; the report includes a count-profile check illustrating the insufficiency of the three line assertions alone.
- The two rational witness caps need not be exact norms. The vanishing BR remainder does not yield a strict margin at equality from a non-strict norm cap. No proof at the sharpened threshold itself or computation of the actual optimal coefficient is offered.
- Subtracting counts of strict intervals without removing a prime at the right endpoint was avoided; the proved loss is at most one. A positive margin is used to absorb it. No onset was transferred from the octic theorem.
- Lean diagnostics while developing the proof: there is no `Nat.lt_floor_iff` in the guessed form; the successful proof uses `Nat.le_floor_iff` for nonnegative reals. `unfold` under abbrev names failed; `dsimp` exposed the affine expressions. The intersection/cardinality rewrite needed the upper endpoint unfolded in its subset premise. `Finset.mem_insert_self` is a direct lemma, not an iff with `.mpr`.
- In the cap refinement, the inequality chain required `lt_of_lt_of_le`, not `LT.lt.trans`, together with typed denominator nonnegativity; `ring` did not expose the flat denominator definition until it was explicitly simplified. During failed elaboration, partial declarations printed `sorryAx`; they are not included as successful results. Replays of the corrected sources exit 0 with standard axioms only.
- No independent proof of the published analytic predicates, proof of RH, or source-first Gaussian/FFT claim. The executable-source check is for the fixed scalar C ports with the declared execution/trust boundary; no native C run was used to justify a mathematical premise.
