6. Actual analytic progress behind the open estimates

The box Stieltjes statement concerns the actual sequence and its actual limit, not an unspecified constant:

sn=∑k=2nlogkk−log2n2,sn⟶γ1*,s128−log128256≤γ1*.

This complete convergence/trapezoid theorem has no RH or numerical hypothesis. It removes one of the five previous analytic premises from the source-linked box moment/PNT chain. It does not establish the remaining Mellin identities or identify this limit with a zeta Laurent coefficient. Unconditional proof.

For the octic kernel, the exact Fourier transform, its value at zero and its derivative are proved. A separate finite Stieltjes argument uses the precise published Brent–Platt–Trudgian Corollary 1, PDF p.4, printed p.743 count estimate as an explicit literature input:

|N(T)−M(T)|≤0.28logT(T≥2π),M(T)=T2πlog(T2π)−T2π+78.

The finite absolute-value bridge is proved for a continuously differentiable f on [a,b], with 2π ≤ a ≤ b:

∑a<γ≤bmγ|f(γ)|≤∫ab|f(t)|M′(t)dt+0.28[|f(a)|loga+|f(b)|logb+∫ab|f′(t)|logtdt].

Absolute value is not differentiated at its zeros. The proof uses smooth majorants √(f² + ε²), bounds their derivatives by |f′|, and passes to ε → 0. The resulting theorem is specialized to the actual scaled octic transform. The infinite tail, numerical moment caps and full zero estimate remain separate obligations. The BPT numerical/source theorem is literature-backed; its original computational proof has not been source-verified here.

A second network reuse extracts the actual inverse-square zero mass from the existing Nicolas spectral bound, using the legal limit x → 1 from above:

The source predicate is the actual summable W(x) bound from Nicolas, equations (1.18)–(1.19), PDF p.4; β from (1.3), PDF p.2. The formal argument proves summability and the limiting passage; the decimal cap is kernel-checked. This reusable spectral fact does not complete the 0.076 transfer.
