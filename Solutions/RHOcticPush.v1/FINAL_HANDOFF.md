The primary results are the octic prime-window bounds, exact Box moments and pointwise bounds, signed compensation, stronger ordinary-PNT functions, quantitative Ramanujan consequences and their actual IEANTN propagation. Their cited mathematical sources are pinned at commit `65a11813c5ba9f7f56ccd6b6aee9c75a3feeb2e8`. Later exposition, review receipts and publication documentation are separately dated additions; they are not claimed to have existed at that commit.

All scoped proof and control gates have actual accepted receipts. The analytic 739-module result combines a validated 114-module source-built prefix from the same campaign with 625 freshly compiled modules. This was a documented interrupted-and-resumed route, not an uninterrupted 739-module run. The original failed attempt and source/object/log provenance remain preserved.

The analytic results are B+D: Lean derivations from RH and the exact named literature propositions, with kernel-checked numerical leaves. B is not RH-only A. The separate fixed prime-count example is C+D under its explicit source/model/compiler/runtime/OS/hardware boundary: verified source outputs are consumed by the actual IEANTN theorem, with a kernel-derived numerical witness. The ordinary compiled execution is an observation, not a formal physical trace. The preserved ordinary-ψ and ordinary-π source inputs and the primary half-weight conventions are distinguished in the reports; no new fixed-height endpoint adapter is claimed.

This release does not establish full finite Ramanujan coverage, general ISO-C adequacy, a physical-machine trace, optimality, or a global best-known-result claim. The monolithic full-high clean recipe is prepared and a bounded smoke was executed; a second complete monolithic high run is not claimed. Official Comparator workflow acceptance is not invented.
