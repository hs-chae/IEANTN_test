# Quantitative primes between powers

The six canonical theorem adapters are in Solution.lean. Five consume exactly
PrimeInterval.v4.optimized_strict_family. The density comparison is elementary
and has no analytic input. Both interval endpoints are excluded.

The strongest cube count is greater than 0.70 n²/log(n) for every real n at least
exp(8), and for every natural n at least 2981. The effective maximum also preserves
the larger single-window lower count at small n. The general integer-exponent
consumer covers every r at least three; it says nothing about consecutive squares.

Proofs and exact inputs are in ../RHOnePrimeEvolution.v1/powers/. The local source
replay explicitly compiles these modules, the new Conclusions and this Solution;
it is a kernel proof build, not source-first program verification. The actual
analytic input is the inherited octic theorem package. No new Fourier kernel,
optimality or global best-known result is claimed.
