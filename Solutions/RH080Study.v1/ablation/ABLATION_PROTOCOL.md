# Prospective RH estimate and representation contrasts

`PROTOCOL.json` is the preregistration, frozen before any experimental inference. It specifies five conditions on two mathematical tasks, two independent fresh contexts per condition, 20 trials in total. This is a bounded matched case study, not a full18cell factorial or a claim of general causal superiority.

Three E0/E1/E2 representations at L2/V0 hold the source facts, numerical information and budgets fixed. L1vsL2 at E2/V0 intentionally adds secondary source assumptions and is reported as source enrichment. V0vsV1 at L2/E2 holds numerical values fixed and tests access to genuine source-first proof artifacts. V1 is not eligible until that actual chain is completed; incomplete PV work cannot be renamed a successfulV1 arm.

Primary outputs are exact theorem strength, whole-real domain, endpoints, assumptions and fresh proof status. Comparison of asymptotic constants does not settle explicit onset. Coordinator discovery and source preparation are outside the scored trial budgets and must not be counted as blind discovery. All original outcomes stay unchanged. Ramanujan is excluded.

Each trial has64GiB memory,4CPUs,12GiB scratch,1200seconds,18000outputtokens and180executoroperations. Fixed budget safeguards and last valid locked prefixes are reused from the earlier reviewed native controller; isolation is not widened. Corpus/representation manifests and actual runtime preflight must be frozen before model calls. Provider sampling seeds are not claimed where unavailable; deterministic20261125080 only controls trial order.

Before the first model call, an eligibility scheduling rule was added: run16V0trials in frozen relative order; the4V1trials wait for a complete checked source-first chain and then use their frozen relative order. This is a declared time-wave confound, not a randomized contemporaneousV0/V1causaltest. The actual host exposes64GiBcgroup memory, swap0 andfour-coreaffinity; cpu.max is unavailable at the user-service level, so its requested quota is not claimed as independently measured. Historical protocols and pre-inference corpus amendments are preserved underhistory/.
