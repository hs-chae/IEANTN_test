## Fixed functional: 0.8 is excluded
Write \(J(F)=\int_{|v|>1}(\widehat F(v))_+\), \(D_A=F(0)-AJ(F)\), and \(R_A=\|F\|_1/D_A\) when \(D_A>0\). CMS arXiv:1708.04122v2 §1.1 (1.3) uses the transform with exponent \(-2\pi i xv\) and continuous real even \(L^1\) functions. The kernel proof RH080.Dual.kernel_compact_obstruction in /source_pv/source/CompactDualVerified.lean proves the strict inequality \(R_{36/11}>4/5\) for continuous genuine real \(L^1\)-Fourier pairs. Its stronger uniform floor is \(100/123\) by combining kernel_compact_global with weak_duality_l1 and ratio_lower_of_dual. This is D, not source-first PV, and is an obstruction to the fixed functional, not to prime-gap theorems.

To cover the full CMS class (no assumption that the transform is L1), let V be the compact step from CompactDualStep. It is 1 on [-1,1], 1-A <= V <= 1 outside; hence G - A 1_ext G_+ <= G V for the actual bounded real transform G=Fourier F. Multiply by q_T(v)=(1-|v|/T)_+, integrate and send T to infinity. The first integral tends to F(0) by Fejer: inverse q_T is the nonnegative unit-mass kernel T sinc²(Tx); its convolution at zero converges by continuity on a small neighborhood and the bound 1/(pi² T x²) outside that neighborhood. Dominated convergence handles the other terms, since V is bounded of compact support and J is finite. Infinite J cannot improve the supremum. Fubini yields \(D_A\le\int F\check V\le \|F\|_1\|\check V\|_\infty\). Thus \(C^+(36/11)\le1.23<1.25\) on the full class, in particular the infimum of positive-denominator quotients stays uniformly above 0.8. The extension just given is written (not kernel-checked).

## Analytic input that would need to change
CMS §5.2.2 bounds exterior weighted primes by the penalty B times leakage, with B the limsup in (1.11) of the prime count over sqrt(x) divided by sqrt(x)/log x. Only the upper bound 36/11 on B is used at the fixed setting. For the above pair, B <= 2.04713 suffices for the target (exact conservative lower approximation to its threshold: 2.0471385631275481). Alternatively replace that one exterior estimate for a fixed witness by a weighted estimate at least as strong; its coefficient must be no larger than F(0)-(5/4)||F||_1 after normalization by 2pi*Delta*sqrt(a). No such prime estimate has yet been proved in this submission. The fixed obstruction is not a claim about the actual value of B or about other analytic arguments.
