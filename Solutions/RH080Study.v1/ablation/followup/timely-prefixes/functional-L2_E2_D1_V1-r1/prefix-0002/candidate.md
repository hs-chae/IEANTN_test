Indeed the generating function in the rescaled variable u is (1-t)^(-1/2) exp(-u²(1+t)/(1-t)); Gaussian Fourier transformation replaces t with -t (differentiate at zero, which is justified by a Gaussian dominator in a small real neighborhood). The sum is Schwartz, real, even, and the rational constant is exactly normalized. CMS §4.1 permits optimization over smooth bandlimited transforms, or one can approximate G by smooth cutoffs: Fourier inversion and two integrations by parts on the omitted Schwartz tails imply convergence of the inverses in L1, and the denominator converges by L1 of G. The bound is asymptotic, not effective here.
