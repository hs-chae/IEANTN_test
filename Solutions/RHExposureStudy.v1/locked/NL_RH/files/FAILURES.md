# Incomplete and rejected routes

1. **Do not extrapolate a finite-height table.** Johnston Table 2's
   final row permits x only up to its stated ceiling. Replacing its
   parameter by an arbitrary larger a or passing a cited limited-height
   result to all real x would not prove anything beyond the hypotheses.
   Our shifted Logan result with fixed c=36 was proved independently
   from the same verified H. Its relative-error floor prevents a large
   unconditional extension by simply using finer cells. The centered
   Brun–Titchmarsh alternative reduces but does not remove that floor.
2. **No unconditional all-large-x bridge.** The verified-height
   expansion used here stops at exp(665). For still larger L the
   dimensionless cost of the floor grows like L^4 and the envelope
   eventually becomes negative even though the actual gap may stay
   positive. A short high-zero tail bound without a density/sign input
   did not bridge to the Axler asymptotic window. No negative theorem
   about the actual gap is inferred from failure of an envelope.
3. **Endpoints matter.** A hoped-for literal real transfer of the
   printed Platt–Trudgian range x>K is contradicted by the exact local
   branch. A last-integer theorem alone gives real coverage >=K+1 since
   the comparison is nondecreasing before the next integer, but does
   not decide the fractional crossing in (K,K+1). Exercise of checking
   just a rounded integer counterexample was insufficient.
4. **Short intervals.** Separate PNT endpoint errors yield an h of
   scale sqrt(x) log^2 x, not the desired log x scale. A first
   triangular window pays the entire absolute frequency mass and has
   leading full-length coefficient 1. The parabola reduces this to
   .93 with proven constants, but still cannot be advertised as beating
   CMS's .88 width beyond the explicit finite band or CMS's better
   asymptotic existence coefficient. Modest quartic perturbations of the
   parabolic weight were sampled for exploration; no certified improvement
   on the parabola was obtained. The numerical sampling is not evidence
   for a negative optimality claim.
5. **Weighted.** Simply integrating the pointwise theta or psi
   absolute error over the whole improper tail loses the extra zero
   denominator; it does not imply our factor <1/3. Adding independent
   error bars for A and W also cannot prove their coupled signed gap.
   Small-x all-real improvement below the stated finite audit was
   not pursued fully, and no improvement is claimed at every earlier
   onset of the imported Lee–Nosal corollary.
6. **Proof engineering.** Default Lean decide did not unfold the
   rational sign tests; decide +kernel succeeded for the explicit scalar
   comparisons and all signed polynomial cells. A separate trial
   of decide +kernel on the actual Std.HashMap quotient program at n=100
   stopped at (count 100).beq 25, and is not retained as a theorem.
   We have a generic source/correctness proof and actual native outputs
   instead, plus explicit native_decide axioms for the two large counts.
   The native C++ finite sieve is not source-first formally verified.
   Linking the first generated Lean C files failed due to a package-root
   initializer mismatch, fixed by lean -R /work/submission; compiled
   rerun agrees with the actual C++ final prefix count.
7. **Source requests.** Some locator guesses led to unrelated papers
   or failed admissions. SEARCH_RESULTS.json is bibliographic discovery,
   not evidence for an assertion; failed retrievals are not evidence of
   absence of better literature. No best-known or optimality claim is made.
