The experiment that was actually executed

The authorized research checkout was pinned at 97c2f6bf91b8ac1eff6a298557c05e25aa1a97bf. It contained 62 node families, 118 versions and 258 conclusions, before the two RH-related families were added. The treatment included all 885 extra source/status files, including failed prototypes, not a filtered “working” subset. The live official upstream commit d60421f10c16f5f6c2cb75f1067a72e24e616391 differed; its source was separately archived and exposed without replacing the research package. This is full-package exposure, including prior derived mathematics, not a pure test of graph representation.

The workers used fresh native sessions, identical gpt-6.2-sol/high settings, identical shared mission bytes and the same pinned Lean 4.34.0-rc2 / Mathlib 71a80585 toolchain. The NL worker had no IEANTN application sources or objects. Each worker had its own Linux user, mount, PID and network namespaces, private writable work tree, eight CPU affinities, 96 GiB RAM limit and 64 GiB scratch allowance. No host-wide access or credential was mounted. Broader mathematical literature remained available through a neutral requester-only broker. The full worker saw the current source package, but the external Git history behind the worktree pointer was not mounted.

Scientific checkpoints were fixed at 900, 1,800 and 3,600 seconds. Technical snapshots were taken every minute; those cross-file snapshots are not atomic submissions. Both final portfolios were separately quiesced and hash locked: NL submitted after 3,684.914 seconds and IEANTN after 4,205.528 seconds. Equal-horizon checkpoints support the time-matched view; final portfolios have unequal elapsed time and must be reported separately. Post-lock proof engineering and synthesis are additional coordinator work, never retrospective primary successes.

A common source-helper schema defect caused early failed requests: the helper emitted kind/value while the initial broker expected url/query. Twenty NL and ten full-arm failures were observed at the first defect census. An additive repair was applied equally before the first 900-second checkpoint, tested through the actual isolated helper, and all original failures retained. The unequal lost effort is a real limitation. Fetch failures, rejected URLs and missing papers are not mathematical failures or proof of source absence. One pair, with this imperfection, cannot support a general stochastic superiority claim.

The host had genuinely available RAM above one tebibyte and separate free disk above one tebibyte, so no obsolete four-GiB cap was imposed. The kernel did not expose memory.peak for these scopes; reported sampled RSS/PSS are lower bounds on the true peak. Read-only shared pages can be counted differently by cgroups and process metrics. Eight-CPU affinity was verified; a requested CPU quota was not asserted effective because cpu.max was absent. Actual proof-run receipts separately record elapsed time and child peak RSS.
