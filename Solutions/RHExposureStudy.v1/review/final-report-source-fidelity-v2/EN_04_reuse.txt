Three mechanisms of actual reuse

1. A hypothesis supplies a typed input, not a new numerical table. The new RH node is the exact Mathlib proposition and is intentionally unproved. The proved adapters supply every required finite-height RH rectangle and every open zero-free half-plane with boundary at least one half. Existing proof bodies then consume those values. The full arm also proves the relevant density index is empty under RH. It does not substitute one half into a theorem restricted to seven eighths, or pretend that zero locations supply counts and moments.

Concrete example: the old reciprocal-ordinate cap 10.3194 is not obtained from RH alone. A new proof uses the existing zero-count contract and antitone Lehman lemma to prove the weaker 11.1404 cap. Because this cap enters CH’s scaled error additively, the numerical input can be removed at an explicit cost 0.821√x. This is a meaningful reduction of assumptions even when another written formula has a smaller coefficient.
