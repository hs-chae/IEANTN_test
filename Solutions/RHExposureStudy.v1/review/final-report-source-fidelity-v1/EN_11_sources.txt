Source audit, failures and scope limits

The source review found four local printed-proof issues in Lee–Nosal v4: a zero-count centering sign, a positive-ordinate versus both-sign sum normalization, the monotonicity word in the quoted Lehman lemma, and a missing height factor in a tail term. The separate source report shows each exact project/Blueprint correspondence or precise absence, the original primary locator, and the concrete mathematical effect. A repaired high-range derivation is retained with its remaining analytic inputs. These observations do not establish that the headline theorem is false, and they are not counted as independent exposure discoveries.

Other negative results matter. A legal fixed-strip one-half fallback is weaker than Schoenfeld. Subtracting two independent PNT endpoint errors gives poor short intervals. The coarse zero-count argument does not recover the sharper 10.3194 constant. The 600-billion slab control fails to certify positivity, which is not a counterexample to Ramanujan’s inequality. A completed finite-source count remains missing on the stated large prefix. None of these failures shows that another method is impossible.
